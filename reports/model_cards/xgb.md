## Known failure cases and notes

- Thresholds are frozen at 95% detection on SOURCE validation; target data never tunes them, so cross-dataset FPR / DR are what a deployment would see without recalibration.
- H1: across datasets the dominant failure is a detection and ranking collapse (134 of 136 cross cells), not always a rise in FPR (91 of 136).
- H3: ablating or normalising the C4 'lab-telling' features explains about 0% of the collapse; per-domain normalisation is worse than doing nothing.
- Trained on GPU, predicts on CPU.
- C9: retrained with a few hundred labelled target flows (few-shot), it is the cheapest reliable recovery across datasets (MCC 0.92–0.94 with 1,000 labels); C11's best fixed action is XGBoost few-shot 200.

## Intended use

Research measurement of cross-dataset generalisation on public lab datasets. Not a production detector: every result above shows it must be revalidated (and usually adapted with labels) on the network it is deployed in.
